Payment infrastructure that forgets on purpose.

Every card processor keeps a ledger of who paid whom, how much, and when — that ledger is the business model. It's also a permanent, correlatable record of everything you've ever bought.

Ootle Private Pay is a third-party payment gateway, built as three smart contracts on Tari Ootle, that keeps only the part of that ledger a platform actually needs to operate — a merchant's public stake, a small platform fee — and structurally cannot record the rest: not the amount, not the payer, not the link between one subscription payment and the next.

How it works

01

A merchant stakes to register

Public and slashable — merchant_registry ties a tier (Basic / Standard / Premium) to how much a merchant has staked, so accountability doesn't depend on anyone trusting the platform.

02

A customer pays privately

private_pay checks the merchant is registered and takes a small, tier-priced fee. The actual transfer — amount, recipient — travels as a native Ootle stealth transaction that this contract, and the chain, never see.

03

Or subscribes, without a paper trail

subscription keeps plan prices public but never reads a wallet's public key — every renewal is looked up by a secret the subscriber alone chooses, so two payments six months apart can't be linked to the same person.
