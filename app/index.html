🔒 Ootle Private Pay

A decentralized, stake-gated payment gateway. Merchants register with a public, slashable stake; customers pay and subscribe without ever linking their identity to what they bought.

This page talks directly to your own Tari wallet daemon over JSON-RPC — nothing routes through a server we control. Run tari_ootle_walletd --network esme locally (see the repo for setup), then connect below.

02 Become a merchant

Stake XTR to register — public and slashable, so customers can trust the gateway.

03 Pay a merchant anonymously

Amount and recipient stay hidden — only the platform fee (priced by the merchant's tier) is ever recorded on-chain.

04 Subscriptions

Plan price is public; your subscriber secret is not — never keyed to your wallet's public key.
