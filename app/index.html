Payments infrastructure without the paper trail.

A decentralized third-party payment gateway on Tari Ootle. Merchants stake a public, slashable bond to get API access. Customers pay them — one-off or on subscription — without ever putting their amount or identity on the record.

For businesses

Merchant Console

Stake to register, manage your tier, publish subscription plans, and claim revenue from your customers.

Open console →
For customers

Pay

Send a merchant an anonymous one-off payment, or subscribe to a plan without linking your wallet to what you bought.

Open pay →

How the privacy split works

LayerContractIdentityWhy
Merchant accountability merchant_registry Public Merchants want a public reputation; stake + slash needs an identifiable address.
One-off payments private_pay Fee only The platform never sees the payment itself — only a small per-tier fee.
Subscriptions subscription Decoupled Price is already public; only the subscriber's link to it is worth hiding.
