Disputes

Refund requests the merchant denied (Storefront.RefundDenied), cross-checked against get_order_info so a request the merchant later approved anyway doesn't show up here. Resolve slashes the merchant's stake and pays it straight to the buyer's account instead of into the treasury - use it when the buyer's request checks out. Dismiss records a ruling for the merchant without moving any funds. Either way keep the [order P/O] tag at the start of your reason - it's how this list knows the dispute has already been handled and won't show it again.
